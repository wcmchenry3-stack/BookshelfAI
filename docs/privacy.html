Privacy Policy – Bookshelf

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What We Collect

  • Account data: your email address and a hashed password, used to authenticate you.
  • Reading data: book titles, ISBNs, and reading status that you add to your shelves.

Camera Use

The app uses your device camera to scan book barcodes (ISBNs). Barcode scans are processed on-device and the camera image is not sent to our servers. If you scan a shelf or book cover photo, that photo is sent to our servers and to an AI service, as described under Third Parties.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, or any other personal information beyond account and reading data.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers protected by Cloudflare. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using iOS Secure Enclave via Keychain.

Third Parties

Book lookups. We use the Open Library API and the Google Books API to look up book information. These services receive an ISBN, or a title and author, and no account data.

Photo recognition (AI). When you scan a shelf or book photo, our server resizes and re-encodes the photo and removes its metadata (including EXIF data such as location), then sends it to an AI provider to identify the books in it. The provider is Anthropic. Only the processed photo and our fixed instructions are sent. Your email address, account details and reading data are not sent.

Anthropic keeps API inputs and outputs for a limited period (currently up to 30 days under its published terms) and, by default, does not use API data to train its models. See Anthropic's own privacy and data-retention policies for current terms. Scans made before 22 November 2026 were processed by OpenAI, which keeps API data for up to 30 days and does not train on it by default. We do not keep your photo: we store only a fingerprint of the processed image and the list of books found, for 30 days.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at wcmchenry3@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.
